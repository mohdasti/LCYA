# Individual Differences Analysis - Final Summary

**Analysis Type:** Total AUC & Cognitive AUC Individual Differences  
**Per Lani's Recommendations:** CDT cognitive effects excluded

---

## ✅ **Analysis Verified and Complete**

### **Protocol Used:**
1. **Total AUC:** Raw pupil from squeeze onset to response onset (all 3 tasks)
2. **Cognitive AUC:** Re-baselined pupil from 300ms post-stimulus to response (ADT & VDT only)
3. **CDT:** Physical effort effects only (cognitive effects uninterpretable per Lani)
4. **Effect Calculation:** Separately by difficulty level (2 data points per subject)

### **Sample Sizes:**
- CDT: n = 78 (39 subjects × 2 difficulty levels)
- ADT: n = 78 (39 subjects × 2 difficulty levels)
- VDT: n = 74 (37 subjects × 2 difficulty levels)

## 🎯 **Key Takeaways**

1. **No Significant Individual Differences:** Pupillary effort effects do not predict behavioral effort effects at the individual level

2. **Moderate Cross-Task Consistency for Total AUC:** Individuals show consistent physical arousal patterns across tasks (r = 0.3-0.5)

3. **Weak Cross-Task Consistency for Cognitive AUC:** Cognitive-specific arousal is more task-dependent (r = -0.1)

4. **CDT Appropriately Excluded:** Cognitive effects for CDT not analyzed per Lani's recommendations
